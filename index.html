Department of Dravyaguna
Chincha
Botanical Name :

  • Tamarindus indica

    • Family Name :

  • Fabaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Chincha

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Imli

  • English – Tamarind

    • Habitat :

  • Grows in tropical and subtropical regions

  • Widely cultivated across India in dry and semi-dry areas; also found naturally in forest regions


    • Morphology :

  • Large Evergreen tree

  • Leaves: Pinnate with small leaflets that close at night

  • Flowers: Small, yellowish with red streaks

  • Fruit: Brown, pod-like, curved (Tamarind pod)

  • Bark: Dark grey, rough


    • Useful Parts :

  • Bark

  • Leaves

  • Seeds

  • Fruit pulp


    • Chemical Constituents :

  • Tartaric acid

  • Citric acid

  • Malic acid

  • Pectin


    • Actions :

  • Cooling

  • Laxative

  • Digestive stimulant

  • Antioxidant


    • Properties :

  • Improves digestion

  • Mild laxative effect

  • Reduces heat in body

  • Antioxidant activity


    • Indications :

  • Constipation

  • Indigestion

  • Fever


    • Dose :

  • Pulp: 5–15 g

  • Decoction: 20–50 ml

  • Powder: 1–3 g


    • Formulations :

  • Chincha Panaka
